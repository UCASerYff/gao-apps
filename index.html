下载说明About downloads

每个应用独立发版Each app ships on its own

始终最新Always current

下载按钮指向各应用仓库最新正式 Release 中的安装包。本站每小时自动核对一次，页面打开时也会再向 GitHub 查询。Each download button points to the installer in that app’s own latest stable Release. This site checks once an hour, and asks GitHub again when the page opens.

签名情况Signing

安装包使用 Apple Development 签名（搞文件见其页面说明），均未经 Apple 公证，也未上架 App Store。首次打开可能需要在「隐私与安全性」中允许。Installers are signed with Apple Development (搞文件 explains its own wording on its page). None are notarized by Apple or listed on the App Store. The first open may need to be allowed under Privacy & Security.

校验Checksums

每个安装包旁都附有同名 .sha256 文件，下载后可用 shasum -a 256 -c 核验。Each installer is accompanied by a .sha256 file of the same name. After downloading, verify with shasum -a 256 -c.
