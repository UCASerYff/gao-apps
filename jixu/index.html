01能做什么What it does

摘自From the GaoJiXu README

  • 四个工作区Four workspaces

    总览、收支、攒钱、冒险。流水与积蓄记录按日期分组展示。Overview, Cash flow, Savings, and Adventure. Transactions and savings records are grouped by date.

  • 日常账本Daily ledger

    日常记账、资产账户、余额调整、转账、预算、收支报表和周期记账。Everyday entries, asset accounts, balance adjustments, transfers, budgets, income and expense reports, and recurring entries.

  • 固定收支Recurring cash flow

    固定收入只生成预测，实际到账后再确认入账；固定支出与权益管理订阅、支付历史和提醒。Recurring income only creates a forecast, and is posted after the money actually arrives. Recurring expenses and benefits manage subscriptions, payment history, and reminders.

  • 攒钱目标Savings goals

    攒钱目标与积蓄记录；已确认的固定支出可记为支出，同一支付单号不会重复入账。Savings goals and savings records. A confirmed recurring expense can be recorded as spending, and the same payment number is never posted twice.

  • 勇者冒险Hero adventure

    装备、技能、宠物、祈愿和成就；游戏资源与真实财务记录分开保存。Equipment, skills, pets, wishes, and achievements. Game resources are stored separately from real financial records.

  • 小组件Widgets

    从菜单或 ⌘, 打开设置；提供桌面与通知中心小组件。Open Settings from the menu or with ⌘,. Desktop and Notification Center widgets are included.

03安装与校验Install and verify

如实说明，不做夸大As stated, without embellishment

系统要求Requirements

  • macOS 14 或更高版本macOS 14 or later
  • Apple Silicon（M 系列芯片）Apple Silicon (M-series)
  • 暂未提供 Intel 版本No Intel version is offered

安装Install

解压 ZIP，把「搞积蓄.app」拖入「应用程序」文件夹。安装后运行一次，再从系统小组件列表添加小组件。Unzip and drag 搞积蓄.app into the Applications folder. Launch it once, then add widgets from the system widget list.

如果 macOS 拦截打开，先确认下载来源，再到「系统设置 → 隐私与安全性」允许打开。If macOS blocks the app, confirm the download source, then allow it in System Settings → Privacy & Security.
