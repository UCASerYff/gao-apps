01能做什么What it does

摘自From the GaoJieZou README

  • 五个工作台Five workspaces

    今天、节奏、健康、足迹、乐园；旧通知与深链仍打开原业务。Today, Rhythm, Health, Footprints, and Park. Older notifications and deep links still open the original features.

  • 专注与作息Focus and routine

    专注计时、手动睡眠、习惯、琐事、目标和统计，用统一的轻卡片窗口记录。Focus timer, manual sleep, habits, chores, goals, and statistics, recorded in one unified lightweight card window.

  • 健康记录Health records

    今日、全部记录、统计趋势和快速事项；全部记录可按类型、时间筛选和搜索。Today, all records, statistical trends, and quick items. All records can be filtered by type and time, and searched.

  • 后台专注Focus in the background

    关闭窗口后专注在后台继续，点 Dock 图标或按 ⌘0 重新打开；⌘Q 保存并暂停后退出。After the window closes, focus continues in the background. Click the Dock icon or press ⌘0 to open it again. ⌘Q saves, pauses, and quits.

  • 乐园Park

    农场、牧场、钓鱼、图鉴、荒岛与旅行列车集中在乐园里。The farm, pasture, fishing, field guide, deserted island, and travel train all live in Park.

  • 小组件Widgets

    提供本应用的桌面与通知中心小组件。Desktop and Notification Center widgets for this app.

03安装与校验Install and verify

如实说明，不做夸大As stated, without embellishment

系统要求Requirements

  • macOS 14 或更高版本macOS 14 or later
  • Apple Silicon（M 系列芯片）Apple Silicon (M-series)
  • 暂未提供 Intel 版本No Intel version is offered

安装Install

解压 ZIP，把「搞节奏.app」拖入「应用程序」文件夹。安装后运行一次，再从系统小组件列表添加小组件。Unzip and drag 搞节奏.app into the Applications folder. Launch it once, then add widgets from the system widget list.

如果 macOS 拦截打开，先确认下载来源，再到「系统设置 → 隐私与安全性」允许打开。If macOS blocks the app, confirm the download source, then allow it in System Settings → Privacy & Security.
