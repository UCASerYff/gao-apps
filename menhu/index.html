01能做什么What it does

摘自From the GaoMenHu README

  • 网站启动台Site Launchpad

    Launchpad 风格排列，拖动排序、组合文件夹；双指横向翻页，可导入 Chrome / Edge 收藏栏。Arranged like Launchpad. Drag to reorder and to group folders. Swipe sideways with two fingers to change pages, and import the Chrome or Edge bookmarks bar.

  • 指定浏览器Choose a browser

    每个网站可指定 Chrome、Edge、Safari 或 Firefox；Chrome、Edge 装上「搞门户助手」后支持账号自动填充。Each site can use Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox. After 搞门户助手 is installed in Chrome or Edge, account autofill is supported.

  • 贴边悬浮窗Edge-docked panel

    左右贴边收起为细条，悬停展开，优先显示最近和常用网站；支持名称、网址、拼音和简称搜索。Collapses to a thin strip on the left or right and expands on hover, showing recent and frequent sites first. Search by name, URL, pinyin, or short name.

  • 工作场景Work scenes

    一次打开多个网站，各自使用指定的浏览器和账号。Open several sites at once, each with its chosen browser and account.

  • 下载中心与原生邮件Downloads and native mail

    在应用内复核网址和保存目录再管理下载队列；原生邮件支持收件箱、草稿及附件，明确连接后才收发。Review the URL and the save folder in the app, then manage the download queue. Native mail supports the inbox, drafts, and attachments, and only sends or receives after you connect it explicitly.

03安装与校验Install and verify

如实说明，不做夸大As stated, without embellishment

系统要求Requirements

  • macOS 14 或更高版本macOS 14 or later
  • Apple Silicon（M 系列芯片）Apple Silicon (M-series)
  • 暂未提供 Intel 版本No Intel version is offered

安装Install

打开 DMG，把「搞门户.app」拖入「应用程序」后启动。需要自动填充时，在 Chrome 或 Edge 的扩展页以开发者模式加载应用内的 BrowserExtension。Open the DMG, drag 搞门户.app into Applications, and launch it. For autofill, load the in-app BrowserExtension from the Chrome or Edge extensions page in developer mode.

如果 macOS 拦截打开，先确认下载来源，再到「系统设置 → 隐私与安全性」允许打开。If macOS blocks the app, confirm the download source, then allow it in System Settings → Privacy & Security.
